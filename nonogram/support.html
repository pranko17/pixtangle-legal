Nonogram — Support

Questions, bug reports and feedback

Nonogram is a picture logic puzzle stitched like embroidery: the numbers next to each row and column tell you which cells to stitch, and every solved pattern stitches one figure into the scene in your embroidery hoop. This page answers the questions we get most often. If yours is not here, write to us — we read every message.

How do I play?

Each number is a piece of stitched cells in a row: rows read left to right, columns top to bottom, in order. Two numbers mean two pieces with at least one empty cell between them. Tap or swipe across cells to stitch them; switch to Cross to mark cells that must stay empty. When a line is solved, its remaining cells get crosses by themselves. The very first pattern walks you through the rules step by step.

Why did I lose a heart?

Every stitch and cross is checked right away, and a wrong one costs a life. When the hearts run out, you can start the pattern again or watch an ad for an extra life. A Thimble hint protects you from your next mistake.

Hints

There are three kinds: Reveal a cell marks one cell correctly, Reveal a line fills in a whole row or column, and the Thimble makes your next mistake free. You start with three of each, and the first visit of every day brings a daily gift of one more of each. When you run out of cell hints, you can watch an ad for another one.

Scenes and the collection

Figures are stitched one after another in a fixed order, so the next one is always ready on the Embroidery tab. When the last figure of a scene is done, the finished hoop moves to your collection, where you can open it full screen and zoom in on the stitches. On large patterns, pinch the board to zoom.

I reinstalled the game — where is my progress?

Progress lives on your device. On iOS a backup copy is kept in your iCloud account and is restored automatically the first time you launch a fresh install, as long as that install has no progress of its own — the cloud copy never overwrites a game you are already playing. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple Account and that iCloud Drive is enabled. On Android the same job is done by the system's Auto Backup, which restores during setup of a new device.

If the game starts empty and you expected progress, do not stitch a few figures first — write to us before that, while the restore is still possible.

Sound, vibration and language

Sound and vibration are switches on the Profile tab. Nonogram follows your system language and ships with 17 of them; you can pick a different one at any time in Profile under “Language”.

Ads

The game is free and supported by ads. Rewarded ads are always your choice — you start them yourself for an extra hint or life. You can review or change your advertising consent at any time in Profile under “Ad privacy”.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the App removes everything it stored on the device. To also remove the iOS cloud copy, delete Nonogram's data from your iCloud storage settings.
