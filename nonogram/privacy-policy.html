Nonogram — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Nonogram mobile application (the “App”), developed by Aliaksei Pranko (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use it on iOS and Android.

Summary

Nonogram is an offline puzzle game. We do not require an account, we do not operate our own servers, and we do not ourselves collect, store, or share any personal information. Your game progress (stitched scenes, hints, settings) is stored only locally on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google: ads between games and optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch for an extra hint or life. To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and limit how often you see the same ad, Google may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • device advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android);
  • IP address and coarse (approximate) location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version);
  • ad interaction data (views, clicks).

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policy. Please see Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices and consent

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested. You can decline or customize consent; you can change your choice at any time in the App under Profile → Ad privacy.
  • On iOS, the App requests permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency before using the advertising identifier. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings at any time.

Crash reporting and analytics

To keep the App stable and understand how it is played, we use Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics (services provided by Google). They collect:

  • crash reports: device model, operating system version, app version, and the technical state of the App at the moment of a crash;
  • anonymous usage events (for example, that a scene was started or finished) together with app instance identifiers, device and app information.

We configure Firebase Analytics not to collect advertising identifiers. This data is aggregated, is not used to identify you, and is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy documentation.

Data we collect ourselves

Beyond the services described above, none. The App works offline apart from advertising and diagnostics, and we have no access to your game progress. If you write to us by email from the App, we receive your email address and your message and use them only to reply to you.

Sharing

When you share a finished embroidery, the App passes the picture to your device’s share sheet, and it goes only to the app or person you choose. It is not sent to us.

Progress backups

On Android, your game progress may be backed up to your personal Google Drive through Android Auto Backup, so that it survives reinstalling the App. The backup contains only game progress, stays within your own Google account, and is never transmitted to us.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are served through Google AdMob in accordance with its policies.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how data is handled (for example, in-app purchases), we will update this policy and change the effective date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at pixtangle.support@gmail.com.
